04 — Creative Direction

A Consistent Point of View.

The creative direction explored how OURO's identity could extend into product communications, campaign materials, launch visuals, event experiences, and strategic presentations.

Each application had its own requirements, but all needed to maintain a recognizable relationship to the product's identity.

The result was a shared visual direction intended to provide consistency across different communication channels.
