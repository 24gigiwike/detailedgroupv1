BRAND IDENTITY

Building a recognizable visual identity for OURO.

A recognizable identity, built around the product.

A visual language designed to give OURO a consistent presence across its communications.

OURO’s brand identity forms part of the wider product introduction, giving the product a recognizable visual presence across its communications.

The identity direction connects the product’s visual expression with the positioning and narrative developed for its launch. Across different applications, the aim is to maintain a clear sense of consistency while allowing the system to adapt to different formats and contexts.
