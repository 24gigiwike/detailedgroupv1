01 — Overview & Story

One product. One connected story.

An integrated communications direction for ORIVS and its OURO personal-audio product.

One product. One connected story.

An integrated communications direction for ORIVS and its OURO personal-audio product.

Introducing a product takes more than a strong identity or a well-executed launch. It calls for a clear understanding of what the product represents, how it should be positioned, and how its story should travel across audiences and channels.

For ORIVS, the introduction of OURO presented an opportunity to bring these considerations together as one communications challenge.

The work extended beyond visual identity to consider positioning, creative direction, the launch experience and the communication of the product’s wider business potential.

Rather than treating each element as a separate exercise, Detailed developed a connected direction intended to give the product a consistent narrative and visual foundation across formats, platforms and stages of its introduction.
