01 — Overview & Story

One product. One connected story.

An integrated communications direction for ORIVS and its OURO personal-audio product.

Introducing a new product requires more than a strong identity or a well-executed launch. It requires a clear understanding of what the product represents, how it should be positioned, and how that message can be communicated consistently to different audiences.

For ORIVS, the introduction of OURO presented an opportunity to consider these requirements as one broader communications challenge.

The scope extended beyond the product's visual identity to include its positioning, creative direction, launch experience, and the communication of its wider business potential.

Rather than approaching these as separate deliverables, Detailed developed a unified direction intended to connect each part of the product's introduction.

The objective was to establish a consistent narrative and visual foundation that could support the product across different formats, platforms, and stages of communication.
