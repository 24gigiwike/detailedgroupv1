The story

An ongoing system for one product.

Detailed Group is connecting how OURO is spoken.

ORIVS and OURO are the named entities. OURO is a personal-audio product. The study asks how one product story can connect a launch, an event experience, and an investor narrative. The three workstreams are one project. A completed commercial result is not part of the account.

How the studio approaches the work.

Clarity

Find the signal.

When the moment matters, strategy has to be spoken with precision. The OURO study keeps one product story, instead of a different version for every room.

Discretion

Stay inside what is known.

Detailed Group treats confidentiality as part of the advisory. This case study does the same with the project: it does not fill gaps with invented results.

Integrity

Keep the story and the work aligned.

The studio’s standard is consistency between what an organization means and what it says. Here, that standard is the link between launch, event, and investor conversations.
