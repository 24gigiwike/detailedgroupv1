01 — Overview & Story

One Product. One Connected Story.

The OURO project explores how Detailed Group approached product identity, launch communications, event experience, and investor storytelling as parts of one connected communications challenge.

For ORIVS and its personal-audio product, the objective was to establish a coherent strategic and visual foundation that could support different audiences, formats, and stages of communication.

Rather than treating each deliverable as an isolated creative exercise, the work considered how the different elements could reinforce one larger product and business narrative.
