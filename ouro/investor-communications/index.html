05 — Investor Communications

Investor Communications

Making the wider opportunity clear.

Carrying the product narrative into investor-facing communication.

A product introduction also needs to communicate the wider business context to audiences assessing its potential.

The investor communications direction was intended to translate the underlying product narrative into a focused presentation of the opportunity, while maintaining continuity with OURO’s identity and launch communications.

The aim is to make the business story easier to follow without losing the connection to the product itself.
