04 — Event Brand Identity

One visual language, expressed across the launch.

The event identity carries the OURO product story into the room, from the stage to the materials people hold.

One visual language, expressed across the launch.

Extending the OURO story into the event environment.

Within the NEXT ’26 launch concept, the event identity brought the OURO product story into the event environment.

The visual direction was intended to carry a consistent identity across the different points where the launch was experienced, from the main stage to supporting materials and other event touchpoints.

Together, the applications show how one design direction can work across different surfaces, formats and moments while remaining connected to the wider product narrative.
