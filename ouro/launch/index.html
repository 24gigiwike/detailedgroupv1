04 — Launch Communications

Carry the product outward.

Launch workstream

Introduce OURO without breaking it off from the rest.

Launch communications introduces OURO to the audience the launch is meant to reach. It carries the product story alongside the event and the investor work. Channels, formats, and launch performance are not confirmed. This chapter describes the role of the workstream. It does not describe a completed launch.

Launch communications introduces OURO to the audience that the launch is meant to reach.

Project note
01

The audience of the launch

The workstream exists to meet the people the launch is for. Who they are, and which channels reach them, is not stated in this account.

02

Beside, not apart

A launch that tells a different story from the event, or from the investor conversation, would undo the project. The point is one recognizable product.

03

Still in progress

No performance is reported. The project is ongoing, and it does not claim a commercial result.
