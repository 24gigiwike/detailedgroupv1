02 — Launch Communications

LAUNCH COMMUNICATIONS

Bringing the product story to its launch moment and communicating the wider business opportunity.

Bringing the product story to its launch moment.

Connecting the launch concept, keynote narrative and campaign communications through one coordinated direction.

The launch communications work explored how OURO’s identity and strategic narrative could be brought together in a coordinated product introduction. At its centre was the NEXT ’26 launch concept, providing a framework for connecting the event identity, keynote storytelling, campaign communications and the wider launch experience. The direction considered the launch as a journey—from building awareness and anticipation to the main product presentation and the communications supporting it. The aim was to make each element feel part of the same introduction, with a consistent story carried across the different moments and materials.
