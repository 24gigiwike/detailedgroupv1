02 — Brand Identity

The story has to stay itself.

ORIVS / OURO

Identity, here, means recognition.

What connects the work is not a shared audience. Each room has its own. What connects them is the product story, and the requirement that a person can still place it. ORIVS and OURO name the entities in the project. A finished identity system is not claimed here.

The central narrative stays consistent. Each format still serves its own purpose.

Project note
01

Named, not decorated

ORIVS and OURO are the names the study uses. The work does not invent a wider brand family around them.

02

Same story, different duty

Launch, event, and investor materials can look and sound like their own formats. They still have to be identifiable as one product.

03

Not a brand book

Marks, rules, and a completed visual system are outside this account. The chapter stays with the discipline of recognition.
