01 — The Vision

One product. Several rooms.

Ongoing study

Present one product coherently, even when the audience changes.

OURO is a personal-audio product. ORIVS and OURO are the names this work is built around. The communications objective is to keep that product story recognizable in a launch, in an event, and in an investor conversation. This is the aim of an ongoing Detailed Group study. It is not a record of a finished campaign.

The objective is to keep the OURO product story recognizable across those contexts.

Project note
01

The challenge

Launch communications, event branding, and investor communications do not share one audience. The work is to keep a single product understandable in each of them.

02

Three moments

The launch carries the story outward. NEXT ’26 gives it a place in an event. Investor communications opens the wider product and business opportunity.

03

What this holds

The account stays with that structure. Product features, channels, and a completed outcome are not part of it.
